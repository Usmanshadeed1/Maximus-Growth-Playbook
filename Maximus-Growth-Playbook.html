Why this works

The Inspection Report Pitch

Be the contractor agents call when inspections come back bad

OpenClose
Do this

Email 20 buyer's agents offering a free 24-hour repair estimate whenever an inspection comes back with problems.

Difficulty
Low
Cost
$0
Speed
30–90 days
Upside
Very High

Why this works

Every inspection turns up problems, and agents need repair numbers fast to negotiate credits before closing. Most contractors won't turn an estimate around in 24 hours, so agents are always stuck. Solve that and you become their go-to. You get paid twice — sometimes for the repair, and again when the new owner renovates months later.

Steps

  1. List 20 active buyer's agents in your ZIPs. Search recently sold listings to find who actually closes.
  2. Make a one-page "24-Hour Inspection Response" sheet: what you cover, turnaround promise, license number.
  3. Email then call each agent. Offer it free with no obligation.
  4. When a request comes in, hit 24 hours every single time. That reliability is the whole product.
  5. Tag the buyer in GHL and set a 60-day follow-up for renovation work after they move in.
Careful
Only promise 24 hours if you can truly hold it. One missed deadline and the agent stops calling for good.

Why this works

Steps

Turn every finished job into two more New

OpenClose

Difficulty

Why this works

Insurance Restoration Work

Water and fire damage — the insurer pays the bill New

OpenClose
Do this

Introduce yourself to water-damage restoration companies and public adjusters in your area. They handle the emergency; someone has to rebuild afterward.

Difficulty
Medium
Cost
$0
Speed
60–120 days
Upside
High

Why this works

A burst pipe under a kitchen sink or an upstairs bathroom leak means that room is being rebuilt — and insurance is paying. There's no price shopping and no long decision cycle, because the homeowner is displaced and wants it fixed now. Homeowners also very often upgrade beyond what insurance covers while the walls are already open, which turns a $12,000 claim into a $30,000 kitchen. Restoration companies do the dry-out and demolition but frequently don't do finish work — they need a reliable rebuild partner.

Steps

  1. List water-damage and fire restoration companies serving your ZIPs — Servpro franchises, local independents.
  2. Introduce yourself as their finish-work and rebuild partner.
  3. Connect with public adjusters, who represent homeowners against insurers and get asked for contractor referrals constantly.
  4. Learn Xactimate estimating basics — it's the standard insurance format and you'll be taken more seriously.
  5. Always present an upgrade option: "insurance covers this, but while the walls are open you could…"
Note
Insurance work has slower payment terms than retail jobs. Make sure your cash flow can absorb the wait before taking several at once.

The Core Logic

Your problem was never a shortage of leads — it was buying the lowest-intent traffic available while missing the trust signals that convert higher-intent buyers. Fifty Facebook taps a month producing two customers is the predictable result.

The fix runs in one direction: build trust first, then buy intent (Local Services Ads, Search, YouTube), and at the same time build relationships that never need ad spend (agents, designers, showrooms, neighborhoods).

Don't try all 30. Finish Tier 2, switch on Local Services Ads, and open five agent conversations. That alone should take you from 2–3 customers a month toward 6–8. The niches in Tier 4 — closets, accessible bathrooms, insurance work, 55+ communities — are where you'd go next to double again, because almost nobody local is competing for them.
